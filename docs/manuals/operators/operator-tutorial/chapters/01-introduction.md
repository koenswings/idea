# 1. Introduction

The **operator** is the person at the school who looks after the IDEA box: the small computer that runs Kolibri, Nextcloud and Wikipedia for the whole school, without internet. This tutorial shows the operator, step by step, how to sign in to the Console, check that the apps run, look after the disks and the backups, and reset a classroom computer's card. None of it needs internet, and none of it needs a technician: when something is beyond these steps, call your IDEA contact.

The teachers' side (opening apps, Kolibri, Nextcloud, Wikipedia) is in *IDEA for Teachers: Tutorial*. The words used here (IDEA box, Console, IDEA Disk, Files Disk, Backup Disk) are explained in its chapter 1.
