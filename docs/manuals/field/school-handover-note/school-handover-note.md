This note comes with your IDEA kit. It says what the kit is, how IDEA looks after it, and how to get in touch if you need a hand.

## What the kit is

This section describes the kit your school has received.

The kit is a small computer for the school (the **IDEA box**), classroom computers, and a school network. On the IDEA box you will find Kolibri for ready-made lessons, Nextcloud for your own files, and Wikipedia offline. Teachers and learners open them from a classroom computer, without internet.

The *IDEA for Teachers: Tutorial* shows you, step by step, how to use the apps in your lessons.

## How IDEA looks after the kit

This section explains what IDEA counts about the kit, and why.

> IDEA counts how the kit is used, not who uses it or what they write, so that it can offer help. A donated kit may move to another school if it stays unused.

## How to get in touch

This section says how to reach IDEA if you have a question.

If you have a question or would like help, write to IDEA through the person who handed you this kit, or send a message to the IDEA support bot on Telegram (@IDEA_SupportBot). You do not need to telephone anyone.
