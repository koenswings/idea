# IDEA for Teachers: Tutorial

The tutorial text is in `chapters/` (01 to 15, in that order; 14 and 15 are the appendices: demo accounts, activating the demo setup).
`build.py` adds a Part A divider (for teachers) before chapter 01 and a Part B divider (for the
school operator) before chapter 11, and writes `teacher-tutorial.pdf`. Screenshots are in
`screenshots/`; how they were taken is in `SCREENSHOTS.md`. Demo passwords are not in this folder.
