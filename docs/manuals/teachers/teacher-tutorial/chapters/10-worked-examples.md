# 10. Worked examples: two real lessons

This chapter follows two real lessons from start to finish: one in a primary school and one in a secondary school. Chapter 3 explains the basics (signing in, making a lesson, Reports). Here you see how they fit together in one class period.

In both examples the lesson was already made, with three videos and then three exercises. You do not add or remove resources. You open the lesson, check it, make it visible, teach, and look at Reports afterwards. To make a lesson like this yourself, see 3.4.

Read one step. Do it. Then go to the next step.

## 10.1 The plan for every lesson

| When | What you do | See also |
|---|---|---|
| Before class | Open Kolibri from the Console, sign in, open the lesson, check the resources and the class, make it visible | 2.3, 3.1, 3.4 |
| During class | Learners sign in with their own name and open the lesson. Videos first, then exercises | chapter 4 |
| After class | Open Reports and see who started and who finished | 3.6 |

Open Kolibri the usual way: in the Console, find the Kolibri card and click **Open** (2.3). Do not click **Start**, **Stop** or **Back up**. If the card has no **Open**, stop and tell your school operator.

## 10.2 Example 1: Grade 5A, add and subtract fractions

Monde Primary School. The lesson in Kolibri is called **Grade 5A Duration Lesson**.

### Before class

1. Open Kolibri from the Console and sign in with your teacher account (3.1). You see the Kolibri home page, with **Classes** in the left menu.
   ![Kolibri sign-in page](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-04-sign-in.png)
2. Click **Classes**, then the Grade 5A class.
   ![The Grade 5A class](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-05-classes.png)
3. Click **Lessons**, then **Grade 5A Duration Lesson**.
   ![The list of lessons](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-06-lessons.png)
4. Check the resources. There are three videos first, then three exercises. The videos are **Visually adding fractions: 5/6+1/4**, **Visually subtracting fractions: 3/4-5/8**, **Estimating adding fractions with unlike denominators**. The exercises are **Visually add and subtract fractions**, **Estimate to add and subtract fractions with different denominators**, **Common denominators**.
   ![The six resources, videos first](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-07-resources.png)
   If a name is missing or the order is wrong, stop and tell your school operator.
5. Check **Recipients**. It must show the Grade 5A class. If it does not, click **Edit**, add the class under **Recipients** and save.
   ![Recipients: the class](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-08-recipients.png)
6. Switch on **Visible to learners**. The switch turns green. If it is already on, leave it on.
   ![The lesson is visible](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-09-visible.png)

### During class

You lead. Learners work on their own screens. Walk the room. Do not do the exercises for them.

1. Each learner opens Kolibri from the Console and signs in with their **own** name. If they do not, you will not see them in Reports.
2. They click **Classes**, the Grade 5A class, **Lessons** and **Grade 5A Duration Lesson**. They see the same six resources.
   ![The lesson on a learner's screen](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-10-student-lesson.png)
3. Say: "Watch the three videos first, in order. Do not skip them." The video plays in the browser; nothing is downloaded.
   ![A video](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-11-student-video.png)
4. Then they do the three exercises, in order. Kolibri shows straight away if an answer is right.
   ![An exercise](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-12-student-exercise.png)
5. They stay in the lesson until the six resources are done, or until you say stop.

### After class

1. Click **Reports**, then the Grade 5A class, **Lessons** and **Grade 5A Duration Lesson**.
   ![The lesson in Reports](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-13-reports-lesson.png)
2. Open **Learners**. You see one row for each learner.
   ![Progress per learner](../../lesson-manuals/shots/g5a-14-reports-progress.png)

What the report tells you is in 10.4.

## 10.3 Example 2: Form 3, variables and expressions

Ndlovu Secondary School. The lesson in Kolibri is called **Form 3 Duration Lesson**. The steps are the same as in Example 1; only the class, the lesson and the resources change.

### Before class

1. Open Kolibri from the Console and sign in (3.1).
   ![Kolibri sign-in page](../../lesson-manuals/shots/f3-04-sign-in.png)
2. Click **Classes**, then the Form 3 class.
   ![The Form 3 class](../../lesson-manuals/shots/f3-05-classes.png)
3. Click **Lessons**, then **Form 3 Duration Lesson**.
   ![The list of lessons](../../lesson-manuals/shots/f3-06-lessons.png)
4. Check the resources. The videos are **What is a variable?**, **Terms, factors, & coefficients**, **Why aren't we using the multiplication sign?** The exercises are **Parts of algebraic expressions**, **Evaluating expressions with one variable**, **Variable expressions with exponents**.
   ![The six resources, videos first](../../lesson-manuals/shots/f3-07-resources.png)

   ![The lesson is visible](../../lesson-manuals/shots/f3-09-visible.png)

1. Learners sign in with their own name and open **Classes**, the Form 3 class, **Lessons**, **Form 3 Duration Lesson**.

## 10.4 Reading the report

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Not started | The learner did not open the lesson | Ask them. Often they did not sign in with their own name, or the lesson was not visible |
| In progress | They started but did not finish every resource | They can finish next time. Note the name |
| Completed | They finished all the resources | Nothing |
| Progress | How much of the lesson they opened | |
| Time spent | How long they worked on the lesson | |

You do not need every learner at 100% today. You need to see that they signed in, watched the videos and tried the exercises. Tell your school operator or IDEA contact one thing you saw, for example: "Most of Grade 5A finished the videos. Three learners did not start."

## 10.5 If something is wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The Kolibri card has no **Open** | Stop. Tell your school operator. Do not click **Start** |
| The lesson is not on a learner's screen | Check that **Recipients** shows the class and that **Visible to learners** is on |
| A learner cannot sign in | Check they type their own username. If it still does not work, tell your school operator |
| Reports is empty | Learners must sign in with their own name before they start |
| A resource is missing | Stop. Tell your school operator. Do not add resources yourself |
