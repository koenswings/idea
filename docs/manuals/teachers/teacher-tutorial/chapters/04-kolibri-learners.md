# 4. Kolibri for learners (try it yourself)

Your learners open Kolibri the same way you do: on a classroom computer they double-click **IDEA** on the desktop and click **Open** on the Kolibri card in the Console (2.1). Show them this once.

Sign in as a learner in a second browser window (private window) to see exactly what your class sees. In the demo school, learners sign in with a username and password: `learner1` with `learner1911!`, and so on (the IDEA default is the account name followed by `911!`; check your school's setting).

> **Note on passwords.** The demo learners have a password. A school can turn learner passwords off or on: sign in as a Kolibri admin, open **Facility > SETTINGS**, tick or untick **Require password for learners**, then click **SAVE CHANGES**. Learners who have no password yet then need one set by the admin; the IDEA default is the account name followed by `911!`.

## 4.1 Do a lesson

1. Sign in as `learner1`. **HOME** shows **Your classes**, **Recent lessons** and **Recent quizzes**.
   ![Learner home](../screenshots/S1-01.png)
2. Click **Fractions lesson**. The 6 items are listed in order.
   ![Lesson](../screenshots/S1-03.png)
3. Watch the first video.
   ![Video](../screenshots/S1-04.png)
4. Open an exercise, type an answer, click **CHECK**. Keep going until the exercise is complete ("5 correct" goal shown at the top).
   ![Exercise](../screenshots/S1-05.png)
5. Sign out. Sign in as `learner2` and open only one video, so the report shows a difference.

## 4.2 Take a quiz

1. Open the quiz from **Recent quizzes** (or the class page).
   ![Quiz](../screenshots/S2-01.png)
2. Answer each question and use **NEXT** / **PREVIOUS**. The list on the left shows answered questions.
   ![Question](../screenshots/S2-02.png)
3. Click **SUBMIT QUIZ** and confirm. After submitting, answers cannot be changed.
   ![Submit](../screenshots/S2-03a.png)
   ![After submit](../screenshots/S2-03.png)
Now go back to the teacher window and look at Reports (3.6).
