# 7. Wikipedia offline

This chapter shows you how to find an article in Wikipedia and use it in your lesson. Everything works without internet.

The **Wikipedia** app on the school server holds the full English Wikipedia: millions of articles with their pictures, the same as wikipedia.org but stored on the server. Learners can open it on their own device too.

> **Practice copy.** The kit also has a smaller **Wikipedia (Demo)** app with **Wikipedia in simple English**. In class, use **Wikipedia**. If two cards on the Console's **Apps** page are both called **Wikipedia**, open one: the library page names the book, and the full one is **Wikipedia** (English).

## 7.1 Look something up and use it in class

1. Open the Console (chapter 2) and click **Open** on the **Wikipedia** card. The library page shows the book.
   ![The library page](../screenshots/W1-01.png)
2. Click **Wikipedia** (English). Its main page opens.
   ![Wikipedia main page](../screenshots/W1-02.png)
3. Type in the search box at the top, e.g. `Fraction`. Pick a result.
   ![Search results](../screenshots/W1-03a.png)
   ![Fraction article](../screenshots/W1-03.png)
4. Follow blue links to related articles.
   ![Linked article](../screenshots/W1-04.png)

> **Classroom idea.** Give learners 3 questions to answer from one article; they read on their own device. Full articles can be long, so point learners to one section.
