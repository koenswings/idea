# 1. What IDEA is

**IDEA (Initiative for Digital Education in Africa)** brings offline computing to rural schools. A small computer in the school, the **IDEA box**, holds ready-made lessons in Kolibri, a place for your own files in Nextcloud, and the whole of Wikipedia. Teachers and learners open them from any classroom computer, laptop or phone on the school Wi-Fi, without internet. This tutorial shows you, step by step, how to use them in your lessons.

> **No internet needed.** Everything in this guide works inside the school. Nothing you do is sent to the internet.

## 1.1 Words used in this guide

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **IDEA box** | The small Raspberry Pi computer in the school that runs the apps. Each box has its own name, on its sticker. The kit's three boxes are **sunny-robin**, **happy-zebra** and **kind-panda**. |
| **Console** | The web page that lists the apps and opens them. |
| **Demo apps** | **Kolibri Demo**, **Nextcloud Demo** and **Wikipedia (Demo)**: practice copies with example content, on every IDEA box. Use them to follow this tutorial (1.3). |
| **IDEA shortcut** | The one shortcut on a classroom computer's desktop. It finds the IDEA box and opens the Console (chapter 2). |
| **System Disk** | The IDEA box's own disk. The apps live on it. |
| **IDEA Disk** | A USB disk or stick that the Console has prepared. It can become a **Files Disk** or a **Backup Disk**. |
| **Spare disk** | An IDEA Disk that is still empty. |
| **Files Disk** | An IDEA Disk for your own files. It appears as the folder **School Files** in Nextcloud (chapter 8). |
| **Backup Disk** | An IDEA Disk that keeps copies of the apps' data (chapter 9). |
| **Operator** | The person at the school who looks after the IDEA box (*IDEA for Operators: Tutorial*). |

## 1.2 The apps

| App | What you use it for | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| **Kolibri** | Ready-made lessons, videos and exercises (on the kit, for example CK-12, PhET, OpenStax and African Storybook). You make classes, lessons and quizzes and see each learner's progress. | 3, 4 |
| **Nextcloud** | Your own files: share worksheets with a class, write documents together in **Nextcloud Office**, collect homework. | 5, 6, 8 |
| **Wikipedia** | The encyclopedia, offline: the full English Wikipedia, and a Simple English one in the Demo. | 7 |

## 1.3 How to use this guide

- This tutorial is for teachers. The school operator has its own: *IDEA for Operators: Tutorial*.
- Each chapter has numbered steps with screenshots. The screenshots use the demo setup that is on every IDEA box: the demo school **IDEA Demo School** with class **Grade 5** and the demo accounts in the appendix (`admin`, `teacher1`, `teacher2`, `learner1` to `learner3`; teachers and the admin have a password: the account name followed by `911!`; learners sign in in Kolibri with only their username). Your school's names will be different.
- **Practise in the Demo apps.** Every IDEA box of the kit has six apps: the school's own **Kolibri**, **Nextcloud** and **Wikipedia**, and a Demo copy of each. Follow this tutorial in **Kolibri Demo**, **Nextcloud Demo** and **Wikipedia (Demo)**: they hold the demo school, the demo accounts and the example content. The school's own apps stay clean for real lessons.
